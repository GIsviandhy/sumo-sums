Sumo Sums

A local two-player web game where players go head-to-head solving basic math
equations in a digital tug-of-war. Answer correctly to pull the rope toward
your side — answer wrong and your opponent gains ground. First to drag the
rope past the win line wins the bout.

Built for the classroom: a simple, no-setup way to turn math drills into a
fast, competitive, physical-feeling game.

How to Play


Open the game on a touchscreen device (tablet or smartboard recommended).
Two players sit on opposite sides, each with their own on-screen keypad.
Both players are given an equation at the same time.
Type your answer and submit — correct answers push the rope toward your
side, wrong answers help your opponent.
First player to push the rope past their win line takes the round.



Note: Sumo Sums relies on simultaneous touch input from two players on
the same screen. It's designed for tablets/touchscreens and won't work as
intended on a laptop/desktop without touch support.
